# Manual checklist

Use a fresh synthetic database and separate browser profiles for each role.

- Patient and front desk. Book an available slot, see it on the dashboard and in the staff workspace, request rescheduling, select a replacement slot as staff, and verify the original appointment history.
- Patient. Enter the pre-visit questionnaire, complete simulated eCheck-In, and confirm the new status. Cancel another scheduled appointment with a reason.
- Physician and patient. Create a lab result, verify it is initially hidden, publish it, then open its notification, trend and download in the patient account.
- Nurse and patient. Create a care team thread, reply as staff, verify patient notification, read the reply, archive the thread and restore it.
- Physician and patient. Request a refill, review it, approve it and verify status changes. Report a medication discrepancy separately.
- Billing staff and patient. Create an invoice with an adjustment, pay part of the balance, confirm the remaining amount and download its receipt.
- Administrator and patient. Grant one proxy category, switch to the linked record, verify other categories are forbidden and revoke the grant.
- Physician and patient. Publish an encounter with one shared and one private note and verify shared note visibility. Publish and download an encounter document.
- Patient. Update contact details and password. Change notification preferences. Check menu and key actions on a narrow viewport.
